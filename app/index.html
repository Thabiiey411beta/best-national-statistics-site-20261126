Inflation is climbing again

Annual CPI, January–October 2026: 3.5% → 5.2%. Fuel is doing the damage — up 42.9% year on year in October, and a litre of 95-octane petrol passed R30 inland for the first time. Food inflation, by contrast, is low at 1.4%.

Source: Stats SA, Consumer Price Index, October 2026 (P0141), 18 Nov 2026.

Jobs: more work, but not enough

Employment rose by 101,000 in Q3 2026 to 16.8 million — but 8.6 million people are officially unemployed, and 63.6% of 15- to 24-year-olds in the labour force have no job.

Source: Stats SA, Quarterly Labour Force Survey Q3 2026 (P0211), 10 Nov 2026.

Economy

Growth is slow, prices are rising again, and the Reserve Bank is holding its nerve. The economy is R8.04 trillion in size — Africa's largest — but real growth has not topped 1% in any year since 2022.

Real GDP growth, 2020–2025

The pandemic crash of 2020 (−6.8%) was followed by a 4.5% rebound, then three years of growth under 1%. Figures are the rebased series Stats SA published in 2026.

Source: Stats SA, GDP benchmarking & rebasing to 2022 (2026). Nominal GDP 2024: US$401bn, US$6,316 per person (World Bank).

Where South Africa's exports go, 2025

Africa takes more than a quarter of exports (26.7%) — the largest single market — ahead of the EU (19%) and China (11%). Exports to the US grew in 2025 despite tariffs.

Source: Dept. of Trade, Industry & Competition briefing to Parliament, Nov 2026.

Power: the turnaround is real. Eskom has gone 550 days without load shedding (since 16 May 2025; only 26 hours of cuts in all of 2025). Its energy availability factor reached 68.13% — a six-year high — with about 4,274 MW of surplus capacity. Source: Eskom operational update, 24 Nov 2026.

People

63.1 million people, a median age of 28.7, and a young country getting older slowly: a quarter of the population is under 15, and 7.1% is 65 or older.

Age structure, 2025

Two in three South Africans are of working age (15–64). The 60-and-over group is 10.7% of the population — about 6.8 million people.

Source: Stats SA, Mid-year population estimates 2025 (P0302), 28 Jul 2025.

Reading the labour figures: the official unemployment rate (LU1) counts people who looked for work in the past four weeks and could start within two weeks.
